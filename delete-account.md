# Delete your BalkanTruck account

You can delete your BalkanTruck account and its associated data at any time.
There are two ways to do it.

## Option 1 — In the app

1. Open the BalkanTruck app and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings**, and under the **Account** section tap **Delete account**.
3. Enter your current password to confirm.
4. Confirm again when prompted.

Deletion takes effect **immediately**. Your session ends right away and you can
no longer sign in with that account.

## Option 2 — By email

If you have already uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in for any reason, email
**[contact@balkantruck.rs](mailto:contact@balkantruck.rs)** from the email address
you registered with, with the subject line **"Account deletion"**.

We will verify the request and complete the deletion within **30 days**, and
confirm by email once it is done. We do not charge for this and we will not ask
you to reinstall the app.

## What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

- Your email address
- Your username
- Your display name and bio
- Your profile picture
- Your ability to sign in — your password is replaced with a value that cannot be used

The account is marked as deleted and cannot be recovered or reactivated.

## What is kept, and why

Content you posted — forum topics, posts and comments, poll responses, and
public chat messages — is **not** removed automatically, because deleting it
would also destroy other users' replies and conversations that quote or depend
on it. This content stays visible, but it is **no longer linked to you**: your
name, username, avatar and email are gone from it permanently, so it can no
longer be traced back to your identity.

This anonymised content is kept for as long as the Service operates.

**If you want your posts removed as well**, say so in your deletion email, or
email us afterwards at
[contact@balkantruck.rs](mailto:contact@balkantruck.rs) — we will remove them
on request.

We may also retain a minimal record of the deletion itself, and any information
we are required to keep by law, for as long as the law requires.
